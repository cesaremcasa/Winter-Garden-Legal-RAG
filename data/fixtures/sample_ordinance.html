Sample Community Ordinance

Fixture section 1. A permit application may be submitted to the clerk during public counter hours, Monday through Friday, from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., excluding holidays.

Fixture section 2. The clerk must keep an application log and provide a written receipt when an application is accepted. An incomplete application may be returned with a written list of missing items.

Fixture section 3. A person may request a copy of the application log by contacting the clerk. The request should identify the date range and may be inspected during public counter hours.

This is an original training fixture, not an enacted ordinance and not legal advice.
